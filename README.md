# 💍 Enterprise Wedding Management Portal | TecTell

> **An advanced, end-to-end full-stack ecosystem built by TecTell to completely eliminate the logistical chaos of modern, large-scale Indian weddings—orchestrating guest intelligence, automated WhatsApp communications, dynamic room/transport logistics, and real-time financial tracking in a single pane of glass.**

---

## 🚀 The Operational Problem
Planning a major wedding is notoriously fragmented. Planners and families struggle daily with messy spreadsheets, lost WhatsApp chats, manual phone call RSVPs, chaotic hotel room allotments, and unmanaged vendor budgets. This widespread operational friction leads to miscommunication, double bookings, and severe stress during high-stakes events.

## 💡 The TecTell Solution
Developed by **TecTell**, this platform serves as an enterprise-grade digital command center. It bridges the gap between event organizers and guests by combining a high-performance administrative backend with sleek, mobile-optimized public guest portals—automating the entire wedding lifecycle from initial digital invitation to final post-event reporting.

---

## ✨ Core Feature Modules

* **👥 Event & Guest Management:** Centralized master database to manage all guest profiles, categorize families, track group relations, and organize attendee tiers.
* **📩 Advanced RSVP Engine:** Comprehensive RSVP tracking capturing granular attendance status across multi-day ceremonies (Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception) with dietary preferences.
* **🌐 Guest-Facing Frontend Portal:** A gorgeous, mobile-responsive web experience where invitees can view personalized schedules, dress codes, and venue maps.
* **🏨 Smart Room Allocation Matrix:** Automated lodging manager to map out guest families to specific hotels and room blocks, preventing double-booking and tracking occupancy.
* **🚐 Transport & Logistics Management:** Streamlined coordination for travel schedules, flight details, airport pickups, and fleet allocation.
* **💬 Bulk WhatsApp Integration (Meta Cloud API):** Direct integration with the Meta Graph API to blast automated digital invitations, reminders, and updates instantly to guest WhatsApp accounts.
* **💰 Budget Management System:** Real-time financial tracker monitoring venue rentals, catering, decor, and vendor expenses against target allocations.
* **📋 Internal Task Manager:** Built-in task board for event coordinators to assign responsibilities, track deadlines, and monitor daily operational to-dos.
* **📊 Full-Stack Reports:** Comprehensive analytics and exportable summary reports covering guest counts, room fill-rates, and final financial expenditures.
